<section id="ltr"><h2>Decode a valid latency requirement</h2><p><strong>Author example:</strong> Requirement=1, Value=10, Scale=010b in an LTR message. Figure 6-15 makes this 10×1,024 ns=10,240 ns. <section id="obff"><h2>Use an activity hint without inventing a command</h2><p>OBFF indicates low incremental platform power cost. CPU Active, OBFF, and Idle identify different platform availability. The endpoint combines those hints with buffering and workload needs. WAKE# and message forms have their own restrictions, and non-D0 functions must not respond to OBFF/CPU Active signaling. A hint is not a new access permission or a mandatory immediate action.</p></section>
