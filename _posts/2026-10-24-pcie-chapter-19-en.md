<section id="equalization"><h2>Connect equalization to the electrical requirement</h2><p>Equalization adjusts transmitter and receiver settings per lane. Start with the initiation rules in section 4.2.4, follow the rate-specific exchange, then check Chapter 8 electrical limits. The first change to each rate at or above 8.0 GT/s requires the procedure unless the specified No Equalization Needed conditions apply to all components. Completion of the procedure does not remove electrical requirements.</p></section>
<section id="retimer"><h2>Add retimer roles without inventing a switch</h2><p>A Retimer is a Physical-Layer-protocol-aware extension device with two Pseudo Ports. Figure 4-61 identifies the segments. It usually forwards traffic but executes specified functions such as equalization phases 2/3. A Retimer (signal retiming device) is transparent to the Data Link and Transaction Layers; it is not an extra bus-numbered switch. Base 6.0 permits at most two per link, subject to additional form-factor rules, and software must not enable L0s on such links.</p></section>
<section id="power"><h2>Distinguish power and test branches</h2><p>L0p (partial-lane power saving within L0) is Flit-only, symmetric in width, and keeps at least one active lane in each direction. L0s is a separate fast power state and must not be supported/advertised for Flit Mode or retimer links. Compliance and loopback branches serve testing, rather than mandatory steps in ordinary device operation.</p></section>
