<section id="topic-overview" class="topic-overview">
<p class="reader-paragraph opening"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="00.01">00.01.</span><span class="paragraph-text">Different storage groups in one device can have different endurance and health conditions. Endurance Group Information reports one group’s warnings, traffic and capacity; Event Aggregate lists groups requiring attention. This report connects per-group FID 18h settings to both logs, following a notification to the relevant data and distinguishing acknowledgement from recovery.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>Endurance Group</dt><dd>A collection of capacity managed together for endurance, potentially allocated to zero or more NVM Sets.</dd></div><div><dt>FID</dt><dd>Feature Identifier; selects the feature queried or configured by Get/Set Features.</dd></div></dl>
<h2 id="main-ideas">The main ideas</h2>
<div class="topic-map">
<article><span class="axis-number">01</span><h3>Storage scope and health</h3><p class="axis-paragraph"><span class="axis-paragraph-number" aria-label="01-01">01-01</span><span class="paragraph-text">Establish Group, Set and namespace membership, then interpret warnings and wear estimates without treating one group as the whole device.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>namespace</dt><dd>Logical storage addressed by the host using an NSID.</dd></div></dl></article>
<article><span class="axis-number">02</span><h3>Units and uncertainty</h3><p class="axis-paragraph"><span class="axis-paragraph-number" aria-label="02-01">02-01</span><span class="paragraph-text">Traffic, command counts and capacity use different units. Work through rounding and zero-as-not-reported before drawing numerical conclusions.</span></p></article>
<article><span class="axis-number">03</span><h3>From configuration to a pending list</h3><p class="axis-paragraph"><span class="axis-paragraph-number" aria-label="03-01">03-01</span><span class="paragraph-text">Select warnings per group, then examine ordering and transfer length. Identifiers, entry numbers and byte positions are distinct.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>entry</dt><dd>One list record; each LID0Fh entry contains a two-byte group identifier.</dd></div></dl></article>
<article><span class="axis-number">04</span><h3>Notification and acknowledgement</h3><p class="axis-paragraph"><span class="axis-paragraph-number" aria-label="04-01">04-01</span><span class="paragraph-text">Follow an AER completion to the aggregate list and individual data. Distinguish notice clearing, entry removal and recovery from the underlying condition.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>AER</dt><dd>Asynchronous Event Request; a host-posted command completed by the controller to report an event.</dd></div></dl></article>
</div>
<p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="00.02">00.02.</span><span class="paragraph-text">Assumes operating systems, computer organization and basic SSD knowledge. Group identifiers, warning values and traffic figures are illustrative, not measurements from a particular device.</span></p>
<p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="00.03">00.03.</span><span class="paragraph-text">Interpret the data first, then follow the notification sequence. Necessary Get Log Page, Get/Set Features and AER fields are explained locally.</span></p>
<div class="overview-connections"><h3>Connecting the main ideas</h3>
<p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="00.04">00.04.</span><span class="paragraph-text">One scenario connects the report: group 7 has 8% available spare against a 10% threshold, plus degraded reliability. Identify the state bits, the reporting mask, how the list selects group 7 and which read acknowledges that group’s event.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>mask</dt><dd>A bit mask that selects individual conditions, including several at once.</dd></div></dl>
<p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="00.05">00.05.</span><span class="paragraph-text">Health data supplies current state and cumulative statistics; events prompt timely inspection. Current data is not a complete historical snapshot, and acknowledging an event does not repair media.</span></p>
</div>

<!-- claim:EGE-HEALTH -->
<p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="02.01">02.01.</span><span class="paragraph-text">EGCW in LID 09h describes warnings when Get Log Page is processed, which may differ from the notification-time state. Bit 0 means spare below threshold, bit 2 degraded reliability, and bit 3 group-wide read-only for reasons other than namespace write-protection settings. Bit 1 and bits 7:4 are reserved. PUSED=100 means estimated endurance consumed, not necessarily media failure.</span></p><details class="source-note"><summary>Sources: Base 2.4 §5.2.13.1.10</summary><p>Source: NVME-BASE-2.4, Rev. 2.4, §5.2.13.1.10, printed pages 238, PDF pages 264</p></details>
<aside class="worked-example"><h3>Illustrative example</h3><p class="reader-paragraph"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="02.02">02.02.</span><span class="paragraph-text">Group 7 reports EGCW=05h, AVSP=8, AVSPT=10 and PUSED=103: spare is 8%, below 10%, and reliability is degraded; bit 3 is clear. The 103% wear estimate does not establish a read-only condition.</span></p><dl class="term-note" aria-label="Terms in this passage"><div><dt>AVSPT</dt><dd>Available Spare Threshold; the percentage used to detect low spare capacity.</dd></div><div><dt>AVSP</dt><dd>Available Spare; normalized remaining spare percentage for the group.</dd></div></dl></aside>

<details class="review-question" id="qa-ege-units"><summary>1. Do DUW=3 and MUW=5 prove exactly 3 GB of host writes and write amplification of 5/3?</summary>
<div data-qa-answer="ege-units"><p class="reader-paragraph review-answer"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="08.01">08.01.</span><span class="paragraph-text">No. Both fields round up in billions of bytes. DUW=3 means more than two and at most three billion bytes. MUW likewise represents an interval, so the ratio is only a coarse observation.</span></p></div>

<details class="review-question" id="qa-ege-mask"><summary>2. Does changing group 7’s FID 18h mask from 05h to 01h clear its reliability warning in LID 09h?</summary>
<div data-qa-answer="ege-mask"><p class="reader-paragraph review-answer"><span class="paragraph-number" aria-label="08.02">08.02.</span><span class="paragraph-text">No. The change controls whether that warning adds an entry; the controller still reports the actual condition.
