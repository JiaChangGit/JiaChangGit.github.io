Match the jitter test to the parameter

Jitter (timing variation of edges or pulse widths) must be read with the rate and test item in section 8.3.5. At 32.0 GT/s a designated Jitter Measurement Pattern is used; 64.0 GT/s distinguishes patterns for different jitter items.
